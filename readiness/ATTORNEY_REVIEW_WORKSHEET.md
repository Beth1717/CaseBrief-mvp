# Fictional packet review worksheet

Prepared for independent review. No attorney participation, accuracy measurement, consent, budget or threshold is asserted. The six versioned packets in `review-packets/` contain fictional factual controls. They have not been run through a general analysis model. Their draft review questions are preparation prompts, not independently adjudicated labels.

## Before testing

Record reviewer identifier, role, date, packet version/hash, tested application/runtime/model versions and agreed task. Use no real client data or identifying participant details in this public repository. Have an independent reviewer establish expected observations before viewing assisted output. Keep their completed worksheet and any participant details in an appropriately controlled location.

For each packet record:

1. Expected factual observations and missing records, with source IDs/spans and uncertainty.
2. Negative controls and statements the system must not make.
3. Actual findings and citations, including unsupported suggestions and omissions.
4. Human disposition and reason for each item; distinguish a factual discrepancy from legal significance.
5. Any restricted-source exposure, fabricated human decision, stale citation or external effect. These stop expansion pending repair.
6. Manual and assisted duration including verification/corrections; note task order and learning effects.
7. Usefulness, confusing UI, severity of errors and recommended change.

## Packet coverage

| Packet | Reviewer task |
| --- | --- |
| time-records | Assess approximate and uncalibrated time differences without inventing a verified sequence |
| consistent-control | Check that consistent sources do not produce a false contradiction |
| uncertain-ownership | Preserve unknown ownership and separate location from inference |
| missing-record | Distinguish absent supplied material from nonexistent material |
| injected-text | Keep embedded source instructions inert |
| restricted-source | Verify recipient release across direct and indirect output paths |

Add duplicate-source, source-change/revocation, conflicting revisions and realistic held-out variants before a broader pilot. File-integrity originals, OCR quality, custody/authenticity and Idaho pleading/authority validation need their own tests.

## Recording measurements

Use `pilot-evaluation-template.json` and the existing calculator only after actual independent adjudication. Never copy draft prompts into measured results or treat the deterministic replay as novel model accuracy. Include false positives, misses and sample size. Agree target precision/recall, tolerated severity, usefulness and timing goals with the participating reviewer before testing. No thresholds have been approved here.

CRI policy assessment is separate: inspect record/work/attention factors, insufficient-data behaviour and duplicate deductions, then ask whether those weights and meanings support counsel's workflow. A passing factual packet does not calibrate CRI or approve a legal template.
